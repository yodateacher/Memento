# MEMENTO

**Memento** is a strict tool for time awareness and self-discipline. It is designed to help you realize the finiteness of time and convert it into real achievements.

## Key Features

* **Memento Mori Calendar:** A visual representation of your life in months. Every square counts.
* **Time Bank:** Earn hours by performing "Assets" (productive activities) and spend them on purchases or "Liabilities".
* **Will (Hardening):** Cultivate discipline through daily restrictions. Track your streaks and don't break the chain.
* **Camera (OCR):** Scan price tags to see the real cost of items in hours of your life.
